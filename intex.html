The Problem

Why do we need better mixed-use urban spaces?

Rapid urban growth creates pressure on land, transportation and public spaces. Residential areas, offices, shops and recreation spaces are often separated from each other. This can increase travel time, traffic and the demand for more land.


Our idea is to bring important daily activities closer together through a carefully planned mixed-use development.

Proposed Design

A simple model of our mixed-use development.

Vertical Mixed-Use Planning

Instead of spreading different activities across large areas, our concept organizes them vertically and connects them with shared public spaces.


  • Ground floor – Shops and public services
  • Lower levels – Offices and workspaces
  • Upper levels – Residential areas
  • Roof – Green and recreation space
  • Pedestrian paths around the development
